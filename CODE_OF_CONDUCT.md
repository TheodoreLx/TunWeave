# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

TunWeave contributors and maintainers commit to providing a welcoming,
respectful, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of identity,
background, experience, or level of technical knowledge.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Respect privacy and do not publish credentials, private logs, or personal
  information.
- Accept maintainer decisions made to protect users, the project, or the
  community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private
information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Enforcement should
be proportionate, documented where appropriate, and focused on community
safety.

For ordinary moderation, contact the maintainer in the relevant issue or pull
request. For a confidential report, use the repository's GitHub private
vulnerability reporting form and prefix the report title with `[Conduct]`.
Do not post sensitive conduct reports publicly.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the
project.
